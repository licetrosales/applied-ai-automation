# Glossary

## ADR (Architecture Decision Record)

A lightweight document that captures an important architectural decision, its context, rationale, and consequences.

---

## AI Classification

The process of assigning structured categories to unstructured content using a large language model.

---

## API

An Application Programming Interface that enables communication between software systems.

---

## LLM

A Large Language Model capable of understanding and generating natural language.

---

## Prompt Engineering

The practice of designing prompts that produce predictable, structured, and reliable outputs from LLMs.

---

## Workflow Orchestration

The coordination of multiple systems and services to automate a business process.

---

## Structured Output

AI responses formatted according to a predefined schema (for example JSON) so they can be consumed by downstream systems.

---

## Router

A workflow component that directs processing based on conditions or AI-generated outputs.

---

## Sprint

A time-boxed iteration during which a team delivers a defined set of work items.

---

## Epic

A large body of work that can be divided into smaller user stories or tasks.

---

## Relevance Score

A numeric assessment of how valuable an article is according to predefined criteria.
